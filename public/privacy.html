Privacy Policy

Life Mentor (Android app com.ragulkumar.lifehub) · Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: everything you put into Life Mentor stays on your phone. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. It collects nothing and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Your habits, tasks, notes, goals, alarms, money records, and health entries (water, sleep, weight, fasting) are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Only you can see them. We cannot see them, because they never reach us.

What the app collects or shares

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. There is no sign-up, no tracking, no advertising ID, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK that receives your data.

Your phone's own Google backup

If Google backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include Life Mentor's data in that backup. That's how your data can come back when you move to a new phone. The backup is stored in your Google account and encrypted by Android. We can't see it. You can switch it off in your phone's settings under Google → Backup.

Backups you export

You can export a backup file from Settings. The app writes the file and opens Android's share menu. You choose where it goes, for example Google Drive, email or another phone. That transfer is made by you, through the app you pick, and is covered by that app's own privacy policy. Life Mentor never uploads a backup by itself.

Voice commands

If you tap the mic, your phone's own speech service (usually Google's) turns what you say into text. That service may go online to do it, under its own privacy policy. Life Mentor receives only the text, reads it on your phone, and shows you what it understood. Nothing is created until you confirm. You can always type instead.

Deleting your data

Delete any item inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you exported are yours, and you can delete them wherever you saved them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts sending any data (for example, a paid upgrade through Google Play), this page will be updated first, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
